MANIFEST - PRIVATE PLAYTEST FOR WINDOWS 11 x64

1. Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All. Choose a folder
   you can write to, such as a folder in Documents.
2. Open the extracted Manifest folder and double-click Play Manifest.bat.
   Keep the game window open while you play. If the browser does not open
   automatically, wait for Ready and open http://127.0.0.1:8080.
3. Register a company. Try buying cargo at your home port, sailing to a
   different port, and selling the cargo when you arrive.
4. To stop, press Ctrl+C in the game window. Confirm if Windows asks whether
   to terminate the batch job. Closing the browser alone leaves the world running.

This copy runs your own world with computer traders. Easy difficulty and
five-second game hours give you time to learn the controls and finish a trip.

MARITIME SERVICES

Open Fleet and expand Maritime services for emergency credit, rescue charters,
machinery futures, privateer bounties, escort contracts, and company pennants.
For local testing, Fleet also has a Debug +100,000 credits button.
Dispatch cards quote naval escort and cargo insurance costs before departure.
After a voyage, check the return-load board for funded freight. Queued legs
carry a 10-credit dispatch fee. Port fees can rise with fleet growth and
repeated use of a lane. A harbor project costs 1,000 credits and 10 machinery
and lowers the base docking fee for everyone using that port.

Pirate boats can raid eligible ships while underway. A solo merchant can lose
up to a quarter of its load and keeps its ship. Take stolen cargo to your
home port and use Fence cargo. An escort reduces the odds of a successful
raid. Insurance pays for part of the recorded cargo loss.

YOUR WORLD

Progress is saved automatically under data next to Play Manifest.bat.
Keep using the same extracted folder to resume. To reset the world, stop
the game, then rename data to an unused name such as data-backup. Start
the game again and register a new company.

If port 8080 is occupied, close the other Manifest window or application
that is using that port before trying again.

If something goes wrong, reply to your playtest invitation with the version
from BUILD.txt, the steps you took, and the visible error or a screenshot.
Keep your password and save files private.

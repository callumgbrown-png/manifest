# Community wiki starter (idea 546)

The in-game wiki (Community → Wiki) builds its port, good and ship pages from the live game and shows player tips.
For a longer outside wiki (Fandom, wiki.gg or a GitHub wiki), start with these pages and set `wiki` in `config/links.json`.

## Getting started
Every company starts with one freighter and some cash. Buy a good where it's produced, sail to a port that consumes it,
and sell. Fuel and the port fee come off every voyage, so short hops with full holds pay best early on.

## Prices
Each port's price for a good depends on its stock: selling adds stock and lowers the price; buying lowers stock and raises
it. Prices never go below the good's floor or above its ceiling. Look at **inbound** cargo before you sail: if many
ships are heading to the same port with the same good, the price will fall before you arrive.

## Ships
Coasters are small and cheap to run, freighters are the all-rounder, bulkers carry the most but cost the most to keep.
Daily upkeep is charged for every ship except one freighter's worth.

## Orders
Buy orders hold your cash in escrow and fill when someone sells into them; sell orders list your cargo at a port.

## Pirates and police
Pirates can raid loaded ships at sea. Waters near your home port are safer; police patrols deter raids.

## Seasons
A season lasts a fixed number of days. The leaderboard ranks net worth. Cosmetics and badges are kept; companies start fresh.

## Cosmetics
Skins, flags, funnels, logos, wakes, horns, banners, cranes and name plates change only looks. Get them free, from
seasons, from challenges and testing, or with codes from supporter packs.

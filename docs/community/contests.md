# Contests and the meme template (ideas 195, 547, 548, 549, 550)

General rules for every contest (paste into the announcement):
- Free to enter; one entry per person; your own work only; no AI-generated entries unless the contest says so.
- By entering you allow us to show the entry (with your chosen display name) on Discord, social media and in devlogs. You keep the rights.
- Prizes are cosmetic unlock codes, sent by Discord DM. No cash value. Judges' decision is final.
- Keep it friendly: nothing hateful, sexual or violent.

## Community map-design contest (195)
Brief: design an archipelago of 20-40 ports. Submit a PNG sketch **or** a JSON file in the format of `docs/archipelago-example.json`
(ports with x/y, produces/consumes; routes). Judged on: fun trade routes (do goods need to travel?), readability, a memorable name.
Winner's map is used for a season (after balance checks; see `docs/ARCHIPELAGO.md`), with their display name in the season title.
Prizes: winner `naming:3` + `pack:pack.founder`; runners-up `naming:1`.

## Fan art contest (547)
Theme each round (e.g. "storm at sea", "my company's flagship"). Any medium. Two weeks. Post in #art with #fanart-[ROUND].
Prize: supporter pack code; winner featured as the Discord banner for a month.

## Fan-made ship contest (548)
Draw a ship skin on the template (a 64x24 freighter outline at 8x, 4 colours + pattern). The winning design is added to
`config/content/community/cosmetics.json` as a reward skin named after the designer's company, and given free to everyone for the next season (source `seasonal`).

## Lore writing contest (549)
Up to 500 words: a story about a port, a captain or a cargo in the Manifest archipelago. Judged on voice and use of the world.
Winners are read in a devlog video and become a wiki article; prize `naming:1` + a badge code.

## Meme template (550)
Template image: two-panel "Buying grain at [cheap port]" / "Arriving to find 6 other freighters". Provide a blank PNG
(1200x1200, top and bottom text areas) and a layered file in the press kit. Post it in #memes with the rule "game memes only, keep it kind".
Weekly favourite gets pinned.

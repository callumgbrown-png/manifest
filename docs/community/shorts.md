# Shorts (ideas 526-530)
Vertical 1080x1920, 20-45 s, captions on screen, hook in the first 2 seconds, game link in the pinned comment.
Data for weekly Shorts comes from the public stats page and the game's history.

## 526 "What pirates stole this week"
- 0-2 s: "Pirates stole [N] tonnes this week." (pirate flag zooms in)
- 2-15 s: the three biggest raids as map clips with labels (good, tonnes, lane)
- 15-25 s: "The most dangerous lane: [A] → [B]."
- 25-30 s: "Sail with an escort, or stay near home." → game link

## 527 "Price crash explained"
- Hook: "Grain just lost 60% of its value. Here's why."
- Price chart line falling while ships arrive at the port
- "Five companies all sold grain at [PORT] the same day. Stock went up, price went down."
- "The fix: check inbound cargo before you sail." (show the inbound number)
- End: "Would you have sold?"

## 528 "Sprite timelapse"
- 20 s timelapse of a ship sprite being drawn, music from the soundtrack
- Last 3 s: the ship sailing on the map
- Caption: "Every ship in Manifest is hand-pixelled."

## 529 "A day in the life of a captain"
- POV narration, one in-game day in 30 s: "06:00 load grain at [PORT] … 11:00 a pirate on radar … 15:00 sell at [PORT], +[N] profit … 20:00 back home."
- Show clock in the corner.

## 530 "The economy explained"
- "Every price in Manifest is set by players."
- Three beats: stock moves price; every port makes and uses different goods; prices have floors and ceilings.
- End on the leaderboard: "Out-trade everyone this season."

# Devlogs, changelogs and tutorials (ideas 185, 186, 187, 520, 524, 525, 531)

## Devlog series "Part N/M" (185)
A fixed 8-part arc, so viewers know where they are. Title format: `Building a shipping trade game — Part 3/8: Prices that move`.
1/8 The idea: one ship, four goods, forty ports
2/8 Drawing the map (islands, lanes, harbours)
3/8 Prices that move (stock, floors and ceilings)
4/8 Pirates and police
5/8 Orders that fill while you sleep
6/8 Seasons and the leaderboard
7/8 Cosmetics that don't change stats (and why)
8/8 Launch numbers and what's next
Each: 6-10 minutes, one gameplay clip in the first 10 seconds, end card to the next part and the game link.

## Before-and-after sprite clips (186)
5-8 seconds, vertical 1080x1920: old sprite (2 s) → wipe → new sprite (3 s) → both side by side, at 8x nearest-neighbour.
Caption: "Freighter, v1 → v3". Post weekly to Shorts, TikTok and #art.

## Player story clips (187)
Ask in #general for "your best or worst voyage". With the player's written OK (company name only, never real names),
replay it with the season replay tool and narrate in 30-45 s: setup, risk, outcome. End: "What's your story? Tell us on Discord."

## Patch note videos (525)
60-90 s: title card (3 s) → each change with an on-screen label and a 5-10 s clip → "Full notes: [LINK]".
Record at 1080p, map zoomed so ships read clearly; captions burned in.

## Pixel-art process devlog (531)
One sprite from sketch to final in 4-6 minutes: silhouette → colour blocks → shading → outline choices → animation frames →
in-game test at real zoom. Show the palette swatch the whole time. Link the sprite pack and tutorials.

## Pixel-art tutorials (520)
Short written + video series, each tied to a game asset:
1. A readable 16x16 ship in 10 minutes
2. Water that loops (4 frames)
3. Flags that wave (3 frames)
4. Isometric-ish cranes and quays
5. Icons that survive colour-blind filters
6. Palette swaps: one hull, ten skins (how the in-game cosmetics work)
Publish on itch.io (free, with tip option) and YouTube; each ends with a mini challenge to post in #art.
